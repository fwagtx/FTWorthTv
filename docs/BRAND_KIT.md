# Fort Worth Texas TV — brand kit

Everything here is generated by `python scripts/make_brandkit.py` into `assets/brand/` (22 files) from the tokens below, so
it is reproducible, free, and contains no third-party artwork. Replace any file with a designer's version later; the pipeline
only reads `assets/logo.png` (the post-card avatar), which the script also writes.

## 1. Identity

| | |
|---|---|
| Name | **Fort Worth Texas TV** |
| Short name | FTW TV in prose; the marks say FORT WORTH |
| Handle (all platforms) | **@FortWorthTXTV** (13 characters: X caps usernames at 15, so @FortWorthTexasTV cannot be used there; one handle everywhere beats two). Fallbacks: @FTWTexasTV, @FWTexasTV. Set it in `config/brand.yaml` (`handle`, `instagram_handle`) and rerun the kit. |

| Location | Fort Worth, TX |
| Voice | A local who knows the city texting a friend: plain facts, present tense, no hype words, no corporate "we". |

## 2. Logo system (`assets/brand/`)

The default mark is **"Fort Worth skyline at sunset"**: a rounded TV set whose screen shows the real downtown skyline as a
solid silhouette, against a navy-gold-orange sunset with a low sun, and FORT WORTH in Inter Display Black under it.
The silhouette is **traced from a photograph**, not drawn from memory: `scripts/trace_skyline.py` walks every column of a
clear-sky photo and stops at the roofline, then simplifies the outline. The source is the classic view from Trinity Park
looking east ("Downtown Fort Worth Skyline.jpg" by DerekAyala27, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons; outline stored with its
credit in `assets/brand/skyline-traced.json`, the trace shown on the photo in `assets/brand/skyline-trace-overlay.jpg`).
An east-side outline from I-35W (Billy Hathorn, CC BY-SA 3.0) is in `skyline-traced-east.json`; rename it to
`skyline-traced.json` to use that view instead. The reference photos live on the `brandrefs/2` branch
(`scripts/fetch_skyline_refs.py`, `.github/workflows/fetch-refs.yml`), all PD / CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA with credits.

Six directions ship as vector SVG in `assets/brand/logo-options/` (light / dark / one-colour, with and without the caption)
and `options-sheet.jpg` compares them at full size and as 200 / 96 / 48 / 32 px avatars:

| Key | Direction | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `b-skyline` | Fort Worth skyline at sunset (default) | the Dallas page's idea, made Fort Worth |
| `d-longhorn` | Cowtown longhorn on the sunset screen with the lone star | Fort Worth is Cowtown; reads at 48 px |
| `e-courthouse` | the Tarrant County Courthouse on the screen | the one building every local knows |
| `g-sign` | Stockyards-style marquee sign, FORT WORTH / TEXAS TV, "Where the West begins" | western, warm, works as a banner |
| `a-horns` | TV with longhorn-horn antennae and FTW on screen | letters stay legible at 32 px |
| `c-badge` | round seal FORT WORTH ★ TEXAS TV around a TV with the star | stamp, watermark, merch |

Switch the whole kit: set `LOGO_CHOICE` in `scripts/make_brandkit.py`, then
`python scripts/make_logo_options.py && python scripts/make_brandkit.py`.

**Using a logo made elsewhere (ChatGPT, a designer):** save it as `assets/brand/logo-custom.png` (square, on plain white or
transparent) and run `python scripts/make_brandkit.py`. The kit then builds every asset from it: near-white background is
removed, the one-colour version is derived as a black silhouette, and on navy it sits on a white disc.

### Prompts for ChatGPT image generation

Paste the master prompt first, then iterate with the follow-ups. Ask for the art **without any text** when a model mangles
letters; FTWTV is added in the kit in Inter Black.

**Master prompt (skyline TV):**

> Flat vector logo for a local news and entertainment social media page called Fort Worth Texas TV. A retro television
> set with rounded corners, a V of two antenna lines on top and two small feet, drawn as one solid dark navy shape
> (#14182B). Inside the screen, the real downtown Fort Worth, Texas skyline as a clean solid navy silhouette, seen from the
> west across the Trinity River: the Tarrant County Courthouse with its clock tower on the left, Burnett Plaza (the tallest,
> boxy, notched top), The Tower with its stepped top, pyramid crown and spire, D.R. Horton Tower, the two Fort Worth City
> Center towers, Wells Fargo Tower, and the Frost Tower with its slanted roof on the right. Behind the skyline a smooth
> sunset gradient from deep navy at the top through warm gold (#F2B134) to orange (#E8743B) at the bottom, with a low pale
> sun. Under the TV the letters FTWTV in a heavy geometric sans-serif like Inter Black, same navy. Centered on a plain pure
> white background with generous margin. No other text, no watermark, no photo textures, no 3D, no shadows, no gradients
> except inside the screen, crisp edges, must stay readable as a 48 px round profile picture. Square 1:1. Do not draw
> Dallas: no Reunion Tower ball, no Bank of America Plaza outline, no generic cityscape.

**Variants (swap the screen):**

> … Inside the screen, a Texas longhorn head facing forward with wide horns that sweep out and turn up, solid navy
> silhouette, a small white lone star in the top-left corner of the screen, on the same sunset gradient …

> … Inside the screen, the Tarrant County Courthouse in Fort Worth (1895 pink granite Renaissance Revival courthouse with
> its central clock tower and dome) as a solid navy silhouette with white arched windows, on the same sunset gradient …

> Flat vector logo: a western marquee sign in the spirit of the Fort Worth Stockyards gateway, a navy rounded rectangle
> with a gold frame and a row of gold marquee bulbs, FORT WORTH in big gold block letters, TEXAS TV in white spaced capitals
> under it, a small navy TV set with a gold star on its screen sitting on top of the sign, WHERE THE WEST BEGINS in small
> navy capitals under the sign. Plain white background, no other text, flat, no 3D.

**Follow-ups:**

> Same logo, no text at all, mark only.  ·  Same logo, white TV body on a solid navy #14182B background.  ·  Same logo in
> one colour, black on white.  ·  Make the horns thicker and the sun lower.  ·  Keep everything, make the buildings
> 20 % taller so the skyline fills the screen.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo-navy.png` | primary, navy cabinet + sunset screen, on light backgrounds |
| `logo-white.png` | white cabinet + sunset screen, on navy / photos |
| `logo-black.png` | one-colour black (white screen, black skyline): print, stamps, engraving |
| `logo-mark-only.png` | the TV without the FTWTV caption: small sizes, favicons, the post-card avatar |
| `profile-light-1024.png` | profile picture everywhere (white disc, navy ring). Also `profile-400.png`, `favicon-180.png` |
| `profile-dark-1024.png` | alternate profile picture (navy disc, gold ring) |
| `wordmark-navy.png`, `wordmark-white.png` | horizontal lockup FORT WORTH / TEXAS TV + tagline, for covers, intros, email |
| `watermark-handle.png` | the @handle pill for the corner of videos and reels |
| `logo-options/*.svg` | the vector sources for a designer or a print shop |

Rules: clear space of one antenna height around the mark; never smaller than 32 px (use the mark only below 64 px);
never stretched, never recoloured outside navy / white / black with the sunset screen; never on busy photos without the pill.

## 4. Type

System sans everywhere (Helvetica / Arial on the platforms; Liberation Sans in the renderer, metric-compatible with Arial).
Card measurements, as fractions of the card width: page name bold 2.85 %, handle regular 2.45 %, headline regular 3.15 %
with line height 1.36. Wordmark: bold caps with 2 % letter spacing, FORT WORTH in navy or white, TEXAS TV in gold.

## 7. The post, in one paragraph

Caption: a plain 2–4 sentence summary of the story in two short paragraphs, no hashtags, no link, no follow line, at most
one emoji (🚨 for incidents). Media: our white card with the logo, "Fort Worth Texas TV", "@FortWorthTexasTV", a one-line
news headline, the photo. First comment: `Source: <outlet> <url>`, the photo credit, `DM us your videos & photos 📲`, and the
presumption-of-innocence line on any wanted/arrest post. The playbook (`docs/CONTENT_PLAYBOOK.md`) has the content mix and
the daily rhythm; the README has the switches.

## 8. Which socials, and why

| Priority | Network | Role | How it is fed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Facebook Page** | the audience; where the Dallas page's format performs | automated from day one (Metricool) |
| 1 | **Instagram** (+ Threads) | same posts, plus stories from the story template | automated from day one (Metricool) |
| 2 | **X** | the wire: police, stations and reporters post and quote there first; the post card *is* the X look; claim the handle before someone else does | automated (Metricool posts to X on the paid plan); expect small reach for a year |
| 3 | **TikTok** | video only; claim the handle, post manually when there is a clip | manual |
| 4 | YouTube Shorts | later, with video | manual |
| – | LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snapchat | not for this page | – |

Cadence once live: Facebook six to ten posts a day, X mirrors all of them, Instagram two or three feed posts plus stories,
TikTok only with real clips.

## 9. Creating the socials — checklist

1. **Facebook Page** (business.facebook.com → Create Page): name `Fort Worth Texas TV`, category *Media/News Company*,
   bio from §6, profile `profile-light-1024.png`, cover `cover-facebook-1640x624.png`, username `FortWorthTexasTV`,
   location Fort Worth, TX. In Page settings turn on messaging (the "DM us" line) and add the email for tips.
2. **Instagram** (Business account): username `FortWorthTXTV`, bio from §6, profile picture, link the Page
   (Settings → Linked accounts) so cross-posting and Metricool work. Upload the six highlight covers as the first story of
   each highlight (News, Events, Food, Weather, Sports, Wanted).
3. **X**: handle `FortWorthTXTV` (15-character limit), header `cover-x-1500x500.png`, bio from §6, location Fort Worth, TX.
4. **TikTok**: username `fortworthtxtv`, bio from §6, same profile picture (video only; the pipeline does not post there yet).
5. **YouTube** (optional now): channel `Fort Worth Texas TV`, banner `cover-youtube-2560x1440.png`.
6. **Threads** comes with the Instagram account.
7. Take the same handle everywhere the same day, even for networks you will not use yet.
8. Then follow `docs/GO_LIVE.md`: connect the Page and Instagram in Metricool, add the GitHub secrets, flip the schedule on.

## 10. Legal lines to keep

- Wanted / arrest posts always carry: *Charges are accusations. All suspects are presumed innocent until proven guilty in a court of law.*
- Stock and Creative-Commons photos are credited in the first comment (`card.photo_credit_on_card: true` moves the credit onto the card).
- Official photos (police releases, city, NWS, Ticketmaster event art) are used as the agency publishes them.
- The page is not a TV station: keep "TV" in the name but say "page" in the About text, as the Dallas page does.
