The sale of the Angels has the franchise's greatest player excited about the future again. Mike Trout 's loyalty to the Los Angeles Angels had become a subject of fascination to some of his former teammates and coaches, never more so than in the leadup to this year's trade deadline.

The Angels were trending toward an 11th consecutive losing season with no discernable path to contention. But Trout was healthy and productive, and many of those who once shared a clubhouse with him began to wonder whether he was finally opening up to the possibility of leaving, even if he did not say so explicitly.
