The National Weather Service has issued a Freeze Warning for Montague, Cooke, Young, Jack, Wise, Denton, Stephens, Palo Pinto, Parker, Tarrant, Eastland, Erath, Hood, Somervell, Johnson, Comanche, Mills, Hamilton, Bosque, Lampasas and Coryell counties from 11 PM Tuesday to 9 AM Wednesday.

A Freeze Warning means that the season's first episode of sub-freezing temperatures are likely to occur.
