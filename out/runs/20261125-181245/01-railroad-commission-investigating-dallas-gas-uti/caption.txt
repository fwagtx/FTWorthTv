A Dallas pipeline company that ships natural gas is under investigation by state oil and gas regulators for a series of deficiencies that may violate state law, possibly exposing it to up to $2 million in fines.

Atmos Energy, which serves more than 2 million customers in Texas, accrued seven alleged violations during a routine inspection, triggering a monthslong probe by the Texas Railroad Commission.
