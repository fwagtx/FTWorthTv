The undefeated Utes can secure a CFP bye and push Devon Dampier into the Heisman spotlight. However, major playoff implications are on the line when No. 2 Utah and No. 11 Texas Tech battle at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas.

Utah ranks as the only undefeated team remaining in college football, marching out to an 11-0 record entering the season finale against West Virginia on Friday night.
