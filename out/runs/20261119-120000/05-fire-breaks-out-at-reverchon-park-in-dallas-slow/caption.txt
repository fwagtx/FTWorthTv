A morning fire in a parking lot at Reverchon Park caused traffic delays along the Dallas North Tollway on Thursday. A large fire at Reverchon Park is sending smoke over the Dallas North Tollway Thursday morning.

Around 6 a.m. Thursday, a significant fire broke out in a parking lot on the far western edge of Reverchon Park. TxDOT cameras captured heavy smoke wafting near the northbound Dallas North Tollway ramp coming from Interstate 35E, causing delays for drivers in the area.
