The first freeze warning of the season takes effect Tuesday night for parts of North Texas, including Collin, Denton and Tarrant counties. DALLAS - North Texas faces a gloomy, chilly Tuesday with scattered showers before the first freeze warning of the season takes effect tonight.

The National Weather Service has issued a Freeze Warning for parts of North Texas from 11 p.m. on Tuesday, November 24 to 8 a.m. on Wednesday, November 25. Tarrant, Johnson, Wise, Denton, Parker, and Cooke counties are among those that will be affected by the Freeze Warning.
