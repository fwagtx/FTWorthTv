A Fort Worth man spent Thanksgiving recovering in the hospital from a hit-and-run crash that left him with multiple fractures and a small brain bleed. Bo Sybert was riding his motorcycle home from a friend's house around 1:45 a.m. Nov. 21 when a car turned in front of him on Cooks Lane and hit him.

He was just five minutes from home. Now his family is hoping anyone who recognizes the driver or the car will do the right thing and call Fort Worth police.
