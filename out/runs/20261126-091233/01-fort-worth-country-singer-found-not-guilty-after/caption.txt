Tarrant County jurors found a local country music artist not guilty following a week-long murder trial. Christopher Blanton Jr., who also goes by the name C’ing Jerome, was acquitted on Friday on charges of murder and aggravated assault with a deadly weapon.

During the trial, Blanton’s defense attorneys successfully argued that the 2025 shooting death of 32-year-old Cody Davontae Scott was in self-defense. They told jurors Blanton believed Scott had pointed a gun at him.
