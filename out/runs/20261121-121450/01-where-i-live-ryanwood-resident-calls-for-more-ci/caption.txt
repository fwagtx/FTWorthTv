I typically start my mornings either walking my dogs — Arlo the labradoodle and Sterling the blue-eyed corgi — or running through my neighborhood, Ryanwood, and its Sunset Hills Park. These quiet mornings are a reminder of how different the Ryanwood I know is from the Ryanwood many people perceive.

My wife, Ashlynn, and I moved to this east Fort Worth neighborhood in September 2021. She was my girlfriend at the time and had spotted what is now our home on a search portal I created.
