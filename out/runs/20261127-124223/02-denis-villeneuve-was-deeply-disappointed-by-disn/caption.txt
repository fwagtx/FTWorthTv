Denis Villeneuve has revealed his disappointment at Disney's decision to release Avengers: Doomsday in theaters on the same day as Dune: Part Three. Doomsday was initially due out on May 1 this year, but was delayed to December 18 — a date Dune 3 had already claimed.

Because of the clash, Avengers: Doomsday misses out on an Imax release, with Villeneuve’s trilogy-ender having already snapped up exclusivity for the big screens.
