Texas Christian University fired head football coach Sonny Dykes, four years after Dykes led the Horned Frogs to a CFP championship game in his first season in Fort Worth. The move came a day after a 38-17 loss to No. 2 Utah in its home finale.

The Frogs' five losses before that had all been one-score games against Power Four opponents, and by a combined 25 points. The Frogs were coming off back-to-back 9-4 seasons when Dykes got a multi-year extension in April.
