A freeze warning is in effect for temperatures dipping near or below 32 degrees across North Texas through Thursday morning at 9 a.m. It's cold! In fact, Tuesday was the coldest afternoon high in North Texas since Jan. 17, with a high of 47° at DFW Airport.

A First Alert Weather Day is in place Wednesday morning and Thursday morning due to the first freeze of the season for many in North Texas, indicating the end of the growing season.
