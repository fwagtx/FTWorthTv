Some Texans support elevating the golden-cheeked warbler. Others hail the mockingbird, nearly 100 years into holding the honorary title.

Of the bills on the docket for the upcoming Texas legislative session, there are two that could ruffle the feathers of the birding community. Shelley Luther of Grayson County and Eddie Morales of South Texas, the bills want to change Texas’ official state bird from the northern mockingbird to the golden-cheeked warbler.
