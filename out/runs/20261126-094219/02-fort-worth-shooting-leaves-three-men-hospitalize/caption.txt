Three men were hospitalized with gunshot wounds on Wednesday morning after a physical fight escalated into a shooting outside a Fort Worth apartment complex. Fort Worth police said it happened around 11:30 a.m. in the 2600 block of Avenue J. According to a preliminary investigation, a man arrived at the apartment complex to meet with a family member.

He encountered another male that he’d had a previous conflict with. Another physical fight broke out between the two men.
