A look at some of the most iconic performances from Turkey Days of the past NFL halftime shows have evolved since the beginning of the league. What began as marching bands and local high school bands has now turned into full productions.

Thanksgiving has featured big performances from top artists since 1997, when Reba McEntire performed at the first Salvation Army Red Kettle Kickoff halftime show. This year, three games will be played on Thanksgiving Day.
