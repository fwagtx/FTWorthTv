After the Deep Ellum shootings, a family says their fight isn’t over as they try to bring Unique Benjamin home and secure her remains. The siblings of a woman killed during a series of shootings in Dallas’ Deep Ellum entertainment district on Halloween night are fighting to bring her home to Iowa nearly a month after her death.

Jazzmin Jackson and Lamaur Benjamin said they are struggling to raise enough money to transport their sister, 28-year-old Unique Benjamin, home. They also said they are fighting to have her remains released.
