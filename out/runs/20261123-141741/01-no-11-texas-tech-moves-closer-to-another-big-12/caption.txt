Tech needs a win or BYU loss to clinch a spot in their second straight Big 12 Championship game as they push for another College Football Playoff bid as well. Malcolm Simmons had another big scoring play; Texas Tech’s defense got four more sacks and the 11th-ranked Red Raiders moved closer to another Big 12 Championship game.

“We know what next week is. Man, we win, we’re in,” coach Joey McGuire said after Tech’s 20-10 win over Baylor on Saturday.
