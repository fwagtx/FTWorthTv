Dallas led by 14 in the fourth quarter. Jake Elliott kicked a 59-yard field goal with 2 seconds remaining, and the Philadelphia Eagles stunned the Dallas Cowboys by rallying for a 39-38 Thanksgiving victory on Thursday.

The Eagles (4-7) got a chance for the win when Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer decided to go for it with a two-point lead on fourth-and-1 from the Philadelphia 39-yard line with 55 seconds remaining. Dak Prescott was sacked, and the Eagles eventually reached the Dallas 41 with 7 seconds to go.
