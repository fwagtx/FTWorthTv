GTA 6 is already breaking records, despite not having even been out for a full day yet. According to StreamCharts, GTA 6 shattered livestreaming viewership records following its release in New Zealand.

Streamers began grinding the game at the earliest hours of the morning here in the United States thanks to handy loopholes on Xbox, allowing them to get a head start on things. As of 12:30 UTC today, the game crossed 6.38 million concurrent viewers across Twitch, YouTube, and Kick.
