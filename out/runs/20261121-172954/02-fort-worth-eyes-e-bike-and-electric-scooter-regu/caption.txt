The city wants to head off potential injuries and conflict between pedestrians, motorists and operators of the electric devices. Fort Worth is moving to regulate the operation of e-bikes and electric scooters in the city, as reports of reckless operation and conflicts with pedestrians and motor vehicles increase.

The city’s current bicycle ordinance doesn’t address e-bikes and motor-assisted scooters. The Fort Worth Police Department is forming a proposed ordinance that would set minimum age restrictions, helmet use, lighting at night, speed limits and rules for riding on sidewalks.
