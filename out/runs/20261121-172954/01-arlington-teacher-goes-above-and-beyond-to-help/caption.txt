William Thorburn’s Fish for Life Foundation awards scholarships to help area students cover the cost of higher education. William “Bill” Thorburn, also known by his students as “Mr. T.,” takes special interest in helping hard-working students reach their educational goals.

52 Faces of Community is a Fort Worth Report weekly series spotlighting local unsung heroes. At the end of the year, these rarely recognized heroes will gather for a luncheon.
