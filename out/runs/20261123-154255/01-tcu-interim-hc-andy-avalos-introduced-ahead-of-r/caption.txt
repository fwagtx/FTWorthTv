TCU needs a win against Texas Tech on Thanksgiving to be eligible for a bowl game. TCU introduced the interim head coach, Andy Avalos, for the football team ahead of the regular season finale Thursday in Lubbock.

Avalos has also coached defenses at Oregon and Boise State; he also played linebacker at Boise State (2000-04). Avalos started his tenure by thanking his predecessor, Sonny Dykes.
