Christmas in the Garden returns to the Fort Worth Botanic Garden with the Winter Cathedral, new light installations and special event nights. This weekend, the Fort Worth Botanic Garden's Christmas in the Garden returns, bringing 22 light installations to a self-guided holiday trail running Nov. 20 through Jan. 3.

This season, the beloved Winter Cathedral will also return after an absence in the 2025 season. The festive arched lightway became a top attraction for holiday photos after its debut in 2022.
