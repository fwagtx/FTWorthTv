Cases involving gender-transition care, CAIR, ActBlue and other contentious issues have been dropped as a new Texas AG prepares to take office. After losing the election and preparing to hand the Texas Attorney General’s Office to an incoming Democrat, Attorney General Ken Paxton and his office have dropped a series of high-profile lawsuits.

The cases span some of the most politically charged issues Paxton’s office has pursued, including gender-transition care, transgender athletes, political fundraising, immigration advocacy and the Council on American-Islamic Relations.
