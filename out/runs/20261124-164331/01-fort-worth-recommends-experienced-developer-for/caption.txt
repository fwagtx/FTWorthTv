The plans call for a 600- to 800-room, four-star or higher hotel. Downtown could soon see another major hotel as city staff have recommended Matthews Hospitality to develop a site just east of the Fort Worth Convention Center.

The recommendation will go before the city council in the first quarter next year. The hotel, on 4.6 acres, is targeted to open in the fourth quarter of 2029 or early 2030, along with a refurbished and expanded Fort Worth Convention Center.
