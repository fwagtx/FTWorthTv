Cooper Flagg scored 24 points and tied a career-high with 12 assists as the Dallas Mavericks beat the Denver Nuggets 120-101 on Tuesday night, moving into the driver’s seat to win their NBA Cup group. The Mavericks can clinch a spot in the knockout round as soon as Wednesday if Phoenix loses at Houston.

The stakes were high entering this game with both Dallas and Denver 2-0 in West Group A play. The Nuggets have never advanced out of the group stage in the NBA Cup.
