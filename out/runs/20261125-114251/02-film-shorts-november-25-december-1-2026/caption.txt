OPENING The Assassin(s) (NR) Hur Jin-ho’s historical thriller is about investigators (Yoo Hae-jin and Park Hae-il) looking into the 1974 killing of South Korea’s first lady. Also with Lee Min-ho, Choi Yu-ri, Ryu Hye-young, Kim Dae-myung, Bae Seong-woo, Park Ji-hwan, Jung Woo-sung, and Shin Hyun-been.
