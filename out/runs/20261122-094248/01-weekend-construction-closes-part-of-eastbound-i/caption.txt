Eastbound I-30 will be closed from I-20 to Chapel Creek Boulevard in Tarrant County over the weekend for construction activities. The closure is expected to remain in place until 6 a.m. Monday, Nov. 26.

According to the Texas Department of Transportation, the mainlines will be closed between the I-20 interchange and Linkcrest Drive until 6 a.m. on Monday, Nov. 26 for pavement operations. Traffic is expected to be directed through a signed detour leading drivers onto eastbound I-20, then north on I-820 West Loop to eastbound I-30.
