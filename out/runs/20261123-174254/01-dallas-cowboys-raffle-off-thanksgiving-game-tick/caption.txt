Donors who participated in a blood drive at AT&T Stadium might win tickets to come back on Thanksgiving to watch the Cowboys take on the Eagles. On Monday, November 23, the Dallas Cowboys teamed up with Carter BloodCare to host a blood drive at AT&T Stadium in Arlington.

The first 250 donors received standing room tickets to this Thursday's Thanksgiving Day game against the Philadelphia Eagles. All donors were entered in a raffle to win two seats for the game.
