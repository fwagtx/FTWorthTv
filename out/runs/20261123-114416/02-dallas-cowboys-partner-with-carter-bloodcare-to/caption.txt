Texas It's the season of giving, and the Dallas Cowboys are teaming with Carter BloodCare to hold a blood drive at AT&T Stadium in Arlington next week. The event, scheduled for Monday, Nov. 23, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., will feature Cowboys legends, player appearances and the Dallas Cowboys Cheerleaders.

The first 250 donors will receive a standing-room-only ticket to the Cowboys' Thanksgiving game against the Philadelphia Eagles. All donors will be entered for a chance to win two tickets to the game too.
