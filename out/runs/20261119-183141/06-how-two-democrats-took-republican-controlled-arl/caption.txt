The year 1969 was marked by historic moments for the United States: The first man walked on the moon, young men faced the first military draft of the Vietnam War and hippies converged on upstate New York for Woodstock.

Down south, another milestone quietly unfolded. The year marked the last time Democrats would simultaneously hold Texas House Districts 94 and 96.
