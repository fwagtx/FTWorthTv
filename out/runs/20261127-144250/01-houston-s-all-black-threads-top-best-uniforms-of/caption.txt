Just because it's rivalry week doesn't mean teams still can't put their best foot forward with uniform swag. Rivalry week is as good of a time as any to bring out a strong uniform, and plenty of college football teams are proving that this weekend.

Houston is one of those squads. The Cougars boast a simple but effective color scheme with their black, white and red palette, and their look for their Week 13 clash against Baylor plays into that sleek ethos.
