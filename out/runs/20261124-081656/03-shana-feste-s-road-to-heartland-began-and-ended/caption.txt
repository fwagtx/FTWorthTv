The massive Netflix hit is a "full-circle" moment, she says. In the new Netflix film Heartland, Jessica Chastain plays Misty Jones, a country music legend from Nashville who drinks too much, shoots out windows, and cuts off a man’s finger when he has it coming.

Apparently, the world wants a female antihero who is good at being bad: Written and directed by Austin-based filmmaker Shana Feste, Heartland opened at number one on Netflix both in the United States and internationally. Not shabby for a film that Feste was told more than once wouldn’t find a wide audience.
