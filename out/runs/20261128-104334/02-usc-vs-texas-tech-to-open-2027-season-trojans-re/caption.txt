The Red Raiders and Trojans will get next season started in Sin City with a marquee nonconference showdown. The 2027 college football season is set to start with a bang.

Texas Tech and USC have announced a non-conference matchup at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas on Aug. 27, 2027. The Red Raiders and Trojans have only met three times in their history, with the most recent matchup coming in the 1995 Cotton Bowl.
