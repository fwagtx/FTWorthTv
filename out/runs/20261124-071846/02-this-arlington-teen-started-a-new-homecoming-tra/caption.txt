Libby Deakyne, 17, created Mum-Making Day to help Arlington High students feel included. Her work earned her a spot on the 18 Under 18 list.

Growing up in Arlington, Libby Deakyne always admired the elaborate strands of ribbon and oversized flowers that make up a Texas homecoming mum. But when she started her freshman year at Arlington High School, she noticed some students weren’t able to participate in the beloved Texas homecoming tradition.
