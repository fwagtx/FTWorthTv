Community Tips

What is a Community Tip?

A Community Tip is any information about dangerous activities or behaviors that you see, hear, or become aware of in your community. Sharing these tips can help keep your neighborhood safe and protect those around you.

CAMPUS TIPS

What is a Campus Tip?

A Campus Tip is exactly what it sounds like—a way to report any concerning activities or behaviors happening on a school campus. Your awareness can help keep students, staff, and the entire school community safe.

WHAT COUNTS AS A TIP?

A tip is any information that helps law enforcement solve crimes, locate fugitives, or prevent future offenses, including:

✅ Unsolved Crimes
✅ Wanted Fugitives
✅ Narcotics & Weapons
✅ Hit-and-Runs
✅ Missing Persons
✅ Robberies
✅ Other Felony Offenses

WHAT IS NOT CONSIDERED AS A TIP?

Crime Stoppers is NOT a substitute for 9-1-1. If you witness a crime in progress or face an emergency, call 9-1-1 immediately.

Additionally, Crime Stoppers rewards are only available for anonymous tips. Sharing your identity or providing information in person makes your tip confidential—but not anonymous—and disqualifies it from a reward.

How to make a tip?

In the event of an emergency, dial 911 and report it immediately.

Crime Stoppers is NOT a substitute for emergency services. If you are a victim of crime, you should report it to your local police department.
