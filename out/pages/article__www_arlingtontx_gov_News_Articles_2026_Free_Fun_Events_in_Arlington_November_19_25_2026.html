Free & Fun Events in Arlington: November 19 - 25, 2026

Published on November 18, 2026

By Office of Communication

Enjoy these free and fun events scheduled across The American Dream City from November 19-25, 2026. Check back each week for a new list of family-friendly activities!

Texas Authors Book Club: “The Raven: A Biography of Sam Houston” by Marquis James

Explore Texas history through literature at the Texas Authors Book Club on Thursday, Nov. 19, at 6:30 p.m. at Fielder Museum, 1616 W. Abram St. This month’s discussion features The Raven: A Biography of Sam Houston by Marquis James, winner of the 1930 Pulitzer Prize for Nonfiction. The book offers a sweeping look at Sam Houston’s extraordinary life and the turbulent, ambitious spirit of 19th-century America. Copies are not provided but may be available through the Arlington Public Library, Half Price Books or online retailers.

Native American Artist Discussion with Nick Harkara

Get a behind-the-scenes look at the creativity and inspiration behind artist Nick Harkara’s work on Thursday, Nov. 19, from 7–8 p.m. at George W. Hawkes Downtown Library! Explore his artwork on display in the Genealogy and Local History room, hear about his artistic journey and creative process, and learn more about the experiences and ideas that shape his work. Whether you're an art enthusiast or simply curious about the creative process, this is a great opportunity to meet the artist and connect with his work.

Lake Arlington Garden Activity Day

Get outside and discover the beauty and benefits of native plants at Lake Arlington Garden Activity Day on Saturday, Nov. 21, from 9–11 a.m. at the Lake Arlington Native Plant & Pollinator Garden, 5500 Lake Arlington Drive. Explore demonstration garden beds and help to restore native prairie and wildflowers across 2.75 acres near the Lake Arlington Dam.

Spice Club

Add some new flavor to your kitchen at Spice Club on Saturday, Nov. 21, from noon to 1 p.m. at Southeast Library, 900 SE Green Oaks Blvd.! This month, presenter Amy Watterman will introduce allspice, sharing its history, interesting facts and recipe ideas. Participants can pick up a free take-and-make kit with a sample of the featured spice and two simple recipes to try at home. Kits are limited to one per household and available while supplies last.

A Cup of Thanks: Thanksgiving Social (50+)

Celebrate the season with a little gratitude, creativity and holiday cheer at A Cup of Thanks on Wednesday, Nov. 25, from 10–11:30 a.m. at ACTIV Recreation Center! Adults ages 50 and older can enjoy hot chocolate, cookies, a festive Thanksgiving craft and plenty of friendly conversation. Bring a friend and take a break to relax, get creative and give thanks with good company! Register here.
