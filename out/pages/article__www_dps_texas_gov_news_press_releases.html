  • Texas Rangers Expand; Adds 16 Forensic Investigators
    November 17, 2026
    AUSTIN – The Texas Department of Public Safety’s (DPS) Texas Ranger Division is expanding, adding a new Forensic Investigators program – a team of non-commissioned personnel who work alongside the Texas Rangers at crime scenes statewide.
  • DPS Takes on HYROX Dallas; Recruits Next Generation of Troopers
    November 16, 2026
    AUSTIN – For the first time in agency history, the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) is sending four teams to represent the state and compete in HYROX Dallas on Wednesday, Nov. 18. The DPS HYROX team is a new effort to connect with potential recruits who value physical fitness, teamwork, discipline and hard work – all values critical to becoming a Texas State Trooper. By connecting with the HYROX community, the department is expanding recruitment beyond traditional outreach methods and meeting potential candidates where their interests and values are already on display.
  • Reward Paid in Tip Leading to Featured Fugitive’s Arrest
    November 10, 2026
    AUSTIN – The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) announces the capture of this month’s Featured Fugitive in McLennan Co. on Nov. 5. A Crime Stoppers reward will be paid for his arrest.
  • Monument Dedication Honors Life of DPS Trooper (Central Texas Region)
    November 6, 2026
    WACO – Today, the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) held a monument dedication ceremony at its Waco District Office to honor the life and service of retired Trooper Daniel Robert Shown. Shown, 66, died on Feb. 27, 2026, from complications directly resulting from a line-of-duty injury.
  • Waco Repeat Offender Named Featured Fugitive for November
    November 5, 2026
    AUSTIN – Texas 10 Most Wanted Fugitive James Jackson Jr. is this month’s Featured Fugitive. The reward for information leading to his arrest is increased to $6,000 during November if the tip is received this month.
  • DPS Modernizes Access to Crime Laboratory Records with Texas CLR Connect
    November 2, 2026
    AUSTIN – The Texas Department of Public Safety’s (DPS) statewide rollout of the new Texas Crime Lab Records (CLR) Connect Discovery Portal is complete. Texas CLR Connect – pronounced clear connect – is a new online platform that streamlines access to crime laboratory records for authorized attorneys in the criminal discovery process.
  • Rapid DNA Pilot Program Produces “Hit” Days After Going Live
    November 2, 2026
    AUSTIN – Days after launching the second phase of the state’s Rapid DNA Testing Program, the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) Crime Laboratory Division announces a positive Combined DNA Index System (CODIS) “hit” in Montgomery Co. The second phase launched on Oct. 15, 2026, after more than a year of training and preparation. That’s the day participating agencies in both Williamson and Montgomery counties began entering arrestee samples into CODIS.
  • Texas DPS Receives Leadership in Police Aviation Award for Fifth Time
    October 28, 2026
    AUSTIN – The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) is proud to announce that its Aircraft Operations Division (AOD) has received the 2026 Leadership in Police Aviation Award from the International Association of Chiefs of Police (IACP). The award was presented during the IACP Annual Awards Banquet in Orlando, Florida, on Tuesday.
  • DPS Shares Halloween Safety Tips for Texas Families
    October 26, 2026
    AUSTIN – The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) urges Texans to take extra precautions this Halloween to help keep themselves and others safe. From choosing safe costumes and staying visible while trick-or-treating to staying alert behind the wheel and watching for pedestrians, a few simple steps can help prevent accidents and ensure Halloween remains a fun and safe tradition.
  • Six Victims Rescued in Bexar Co. Human Trafficking Operation (DPS Central Texas Region)
    October 23, 2026
    SAN ANTONIO – The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) Criminal Investigations Division (CID) rescued six victims following a human trafficking sting operation in Bexar Co. yesterday.
  • DPS Urges Drivers to Prioritize School Bus Safety During National School Bus Safety Week
    October 19, 2026
    AUSTIN – The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) reminds drivers to slow down, stay alert and stop for school buses as communities across the nation recognize National School Bus Safety Week, October 19–23, 2026. Millions of children ride school buses every day, making school bus safety a shared responsibility for drivers, parents, school personnel and communities.
  • Authorities Seek New Tips in El Paso Mother’s Cold Case Murder
    October 16, 2026
    AUSTIN – The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) is seeking information about the 1995 murder of Celia Loya, 30, on El Paso’s east side. The reward for information leading to the arrest of those responsible for her death is now increased to $6,000 if the tip is received before the next featured Texas Rangers cold case is announced. Investigators are asking anyone with information about the case to come forward.
  • Texas DPS Leadership Recognizes National Top Trooper Team Win
    October 15, 2026
    AUSTIN – Today, the members of the Public Safety Commission (PSC) honored two Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) Troopers for bringing home the top team trophy at this year’s National Top Trooper Competition. Trooper Melanie Moreno (Seguin) and Trooper Brody Telfer (Belton) were at the meeting to be recognized. The pair was named the top team overall at the second annual National Top Trooper Competition in Columbus, Ohio, last month.
  • DPS Honors Courage and Dedication During October PSC Meeting
