CAPTURE DETAILS:

James Michael JACKSON, Jr., a Texas 10 Most Wanted Sex Offender, was captured on November 5, 2026, in Waco, Texas. Following up on tip information, members of the U.S. Marshals Lone Star Fugitive Task Force from Waco, including Texas DPS Special Agents, located and arrested JACKSON at a residence. JACKSON has an extensive criminal history consisting of convictions for Assault Causes Bodily Injury to Family Member, Burglary of Vehicle, Theft of Property, Failure to Identify, and Possession of a Controlled Substance. JACKSON had been wanted since August 2026, after the Texas Board of Pardons and Paroles issued a warrant for his arrest for Parole Violation (Original Offense: Theft $150,000 to $300,000). Additionally, during the same month, a warrant was issued out of McLennan County for his arrest for Robbery. JACKSON’s capture was the result of tip information received through Texas Crime Stoppers, so a reward will be paid.

To be eligible for the cash rewards, tipsters must provide information to authorities using one of the following methods:
• Call the Texas Crime Stoppers hotline at 1-800-252-TIPS (8477)
• Submit a web tip through the Texas 10 Most Wanted website or Facebook page
• Submit a tip through the DPS Mobile App
All tips are anonymous – regardless of how they are submitted, and tipsters will be provided a tip number instead of using a name.
Callers’ anonymity is guaranteed by law and you could earn up to the CASH REWARD amount listed for each fugitive.
These fugitives should be considered Armed and Dangerous! Never try to apprehend a fugitive yourself.
