2026 Thanksgiving Closings and Service Schedules in Arlington

Published on November 17, 2026

By Office of Communication

City of Arlington municipal offices and facilities will be closed on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 in observance of Thanksgiving holiday.

Residential and commercial garbage and recycling collection will not occur on Thursday, Nov. 26.

Residents whose regularly scheduled garbage and recycling collection day falls on Nov. 26 and Nov. 27 will have their curbside collection shifted by one day because of the holiday. That means residents who normally set out their bins curbside for collection on Thursdays will set their bins out for collection on Friday, Nov. 27. Residents whose garbage and recycling collection day is on Fridays will set their bins out for collection on Saturday, Nov. 28. Residential garbage and recycling collections will resume on their normal schedules on Monday, Nov. 30.

Regular office hours and service schedules for some facilities will resume at 8 a.m. Monday, Nov. 30.
