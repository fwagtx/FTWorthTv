Arlington Announces Kirk Franklin’s 150th Holiday Celebration Concert

Published on November 16, 2026

By Office of Communication

Arlington is turning up the holiday cheer for its 150th anniversary with "150 Voices: A Holiday Celebration with Kirk Franklin," a high‑energy, family-friendly concert set for Sunday, December 13 at 5 p.m. at the Dr. Marcelo Cavazos Center for the Visual and Performing Arts, 900 E. Sanford Street.

The celebration features Kirk Franklin, a multi–Grammy Award-winning artist, producer and trailblazer known for revolutionizing contemporary gospel music with his dynamic sound and inspiring performances. Franklin will perform alongside a 150 Voices Choir, featuring 25 students from each of Arlington ISD’s six high schools: Arlington High, Bowie High, Lamar High, Martin High, Sam Houston High and Seguin High. 

Tickets are on sale now for $12 and will be limited. Families of choir members received access to a 72-hour presale window before tickets open to the general public.

Buy Tickets

Proceeds will go to Arlington ISD's Fine Arts Department. The concert is underwritten by the Arlington Tomorrow Foundation and presented in conjunction with the Franklin Imagine Group and the Arlington Independent School District. Together, these partners aim to spotlight Arlington’s vibrant arts community while commemorating the city’s historic milestone year.

Visit Arlington150.com for more historical articles, photos and events planned throughout 2026.
